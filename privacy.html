English

Mochi Nook is an offline virtual pet for Apple Watch. It does not require an account or connect to a developer-operated server. The app does not collect, transmit, sell, or use your information for advertising. No analytics, advertising, or third-party tracking SDK is included.

The name you give your pet, its progress, care history, outfits, and preferences are stored locally on your watch. The app shares that local data with its own watch-face widget extension through an Apple App Group. This does not send it to another person or to our servers. A local previous-save backup and internal recovery copies may be retained on the same device.

There is no cloud backup or account recovery service. Deleting the app may delete your pet and its progress. Starting a new friendship replaces the active save; it is not a secure erasure feature for all internal recovery copies. To remove app data from the watch, uninstall the app. Device-level backups and removal behavior are controlled by Apple.

Purchases, downloads, updates, and any associated payment information are handled by Apple under Apple's terms and privacy policy. Mochi Nook does not receive or store your card details. Optional system diagnostics and device backups are controlled by Apple and your device settings, not by an analytics service in this app.

Support email and this website

If you email support, we receive the address and information you choose to include and use them to respond to your request. This information is separate from the offline app. You can contact us to request deletion of your support correspondence, subject to any legal retention requirements.

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub logs visitors' IP addresses for security purposes under its privacy statement. We have not added analytics, advertising, tracking scripts, or forms to this website.

Questions about this policy: jay212315@gmail.com.
